Agreement No. C23/2025

Buyer: Tidewater Beverages Pvt. Ltd.
Supplier: Ullal Cold Storage Pvt. Ltd.

This agreement is valid from 1 April 2025 to March 31st, 2026.

1. Pricing
1.1 The Supplier shall supply corrugated cartons at a unit price of 41.08 rupees per unit.

2. Delivery and freight
A freight surcharge of 2% applies to orders below 500 units.

3. Payment
Payment terms are net 45 days; overdue amounts attract interest at 1.5% per month.
