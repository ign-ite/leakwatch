RATE CONTRACT
Agreement No. C12/2025

Buyer: Tidewater Beverages Pvt. Ltd.
Supplier: Shimoga Paper Board Pvt. Ltd.

This agreement is valid from April 1st, 2025 to 31 March 2026.

1. Pricing
1.1 The Supplier shall supply rolls of shrink film at a unit price of ₹1,340.28 per unit.
1.2 The agreed price for corrugated cartons is Rs. 35.36 per unit.
1.3 The agreed price for printed labels (per 1000) is ₹419.40 per unit.

2. Delivery and freight
A freight surcharge of 2% applies to orders below 500 units.

3. Payment
Payment terms are net 45 days; overdue amounts attract interest at 1.5% per month.
